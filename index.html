About个人简介

Sisi Zheng is an attending physician and associate researcher at Beijing Anding Hospital, Capital Medical University, working in integrated Chinese and Western medicine (psychiatry). She received her PhD in Integrated Chinese and Western Clinical Medicine from Capital Medical University (2023, advisor Prof. Hongxiao Jia), and her MMed (2020) and BMed (2017) from Beijing University of Chinese Medicine. Her research focuses on the mechanisms and treatment of depersonalization-derealization disorder, acupuncture and neuromodulation, and neuroimaging of psychiatric disorders. She is PI of a National Natural Science Foundation of China Young Scientists grant.

郑思思，首都医科大学附属北京安定医院主治医师、副研究员，从事中西医结合临床医学（精神病学方向）工作。2023年获首都医科大学中西医结合临床博士学位（导师贾竑晓教授），此前于北京中医药大学获全科医学（中医）硕士（2020）及中医学学士（2017）学位。主要研究方向为人格解体-现实解体障碍的机制与治疗、针刺与神经调控、精神障碍神经影像。主持国家自然科学基金青年科学基金等项目。
